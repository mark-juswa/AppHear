# Recognition V3 — Continuous Recognition Reliability (2026-10-17)

Branch `claude/s2-r3-eval-suite`. Nothing is committed. Nothing in Android, the frozen packages
(Candidate01, S2-R2, Gate-2 WORD), the production decoder, the materializer, SentenceBuilder,
FINGERSPELL or Context Assist changed. Protected TEST, E6 and Tier 3 were not opened.

All phone numbers come from development-exposed recordings: one Xiaomi phone, a design signer
plus two declared distinct signers. They are engineering evidence, not accuracy estimates.

**Outcome in one paragraph.**
- **Confirm-boundary rule.** Its contribution is real and cheap:
  - +27 correct continuous words on held-out signers and +4 on the phone;
  - no new confirmed non-sign events on the phone;
  - its extra CAL events are almost all fragments of false activity S2 already produced.
- **Whole-event admission** cannot reject ordinary movement without losing words. No commit-time
  evidence separates them on held-out data.
- **Retraining S2-R3** with the reviewed phone sessions (leave-one-session-out) cut confirmed
  non-sign events from 9 to 4, but cut correct words from 14 to 9. With this little data, S2
  quiets the whole phone domain.
- **The best configuration** available today is Arm A e10 + confirm-boundary.
  - Ungated: 14/23 correct, 9 non-sign events.
  - With the previous milestone's held-out-calibrated confidence gate as an optional precision
    setting (16 of 19 reviewed correct words kept; confirmed non-sign events 10 → 3), it beats
    production on correct words and on unwanted words in every evidence set.
- **The next limiter** is evidence, not algorithms: hands-visible non-sign and continuous signing
  from several people, reviewed blind.

---

## 1. Approach and why

The immediate objective was to keep the consecutive-sign gains of S2-R3 Arm A + confirm-boundary
(8 → 14 of 23 correct phone words) while removing the extra events during ordinary movement
(5 → 9 confirmed non-sign events). There were three candidate levers:

| Lever | Question | How it was tested |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm-boundary rule | What does it actually change, event by event? | Same S2 and WORD with and without the rule; every changed event attributed (CAL, held-out DEV, phone) |
| Whole-event admission | Does a completed event carry enough evidence to reject non-sign? | Perception, S2, motion and both recognizers' evidence per event; calibrated on CAL / held-out calibration signers, validated on held-out signers and the phone |
| S2-R3 training evidence | Does adding reviewed hands-visible phone material fix discrimination without losing consecutive signs? | Arm A recipe plus reviewed phone sessions ("Arm P"), scored leave-one-session-out so no session is scored by a model that trained on it |

All three run through one event-level harness (`eval/runtime_diagnostics/event_reliability.py`).
It replays any S2 package and decoder variant through the reference lifecycle and the runtime
materializer, and classifies every window with the frozen recognizer (displayed) and WORD v2
(shadow). Each event is labelled only from human review:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `GENUINE` | Credited one-to-one to a reviewed genuine sign |
| `DUPLICATE` | A second event on a sign that already has one |
| `CONFIRMED_NONSIGN` | The event lies inside a reviewed non-sign interval |
| `PARTIAL_NONSIGN` | Overlaps a non-sign interval but is not inside it |
| `UNCERTAIN` | Overlaps a sign the reviewer marked uncertain |
| `UNREVIEWED` | Anything else. Never counted as a false word |

The protocol-only shadow session 022535Z is scored per block from the protocol's expected signs.

- **Reproduction** (`build_ledgers.py`, check printed per configuration). The harness reproduces
  the recorded numbers exactly:
  - Tier-2 production: 8/23 correct words, 5 events inside confirmed non-sign.
  - Arm A e10: 13/23, 9 non-sign events, 33 events.
  - Arm A e10 + confirm-boundary: 14/23, 9, 34.
  - Held-out continuous correct words: 9.6% / 15.4% / 18.7%.
- **Material inconsistency found (it changes a decision input).** The held-out DEV "extra events"
  were read as ordinary-movement false events in earlier reports. They are not:
  - 72 of 82 lie within one observation of a governed sign span;
  - 37 output the adjacent sign's gloss.

  They are onset/release fragments of real signs outside the automatic span. Held-out DEV
  therefore cannot measure non-sign rejection. In this report those events are called
  "fragments", and hands-visible non-sign evidence comes only from CAL P1 (reviewed) and the
  phone.

## 3. What the confirm-boundary rule contributes

Same S2 (Arm A e10) and same WORD (frozen); only the decoder differs.
Source: `cb_contribution.py` → `cb_contribution.json`.

| Evidence | Production decoder | + confirm-boundary | What changed |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Held-out DEV continuous, correct words (of 812) | 125 | **152** | +27; 1 duplicate; merged events 100 → 79 |
| Held-out DEV natural, correct words (of 812) | 236 | 238 | ~unchanged (rest separates signs already) |
| CAL distinct signs (of 1,980) | 1,461 | 1,498 | +37; 7 within-sign false splits (0.5%) |
| CAL events on non-sign blocks | 85 | 96 | 19 pieces of 12 already-false events; 3 sign-tail pieces; **1 new activation** |
| Phone reviewed sessions, correct words | 15 | **19** | New events: SEP27 HOME, 023004Z B-HOME. Cleaner windows: 023004Z B-HELLO, C-HELLO |
| Phone confirmed non-sign events | 10 | 10 | none added |
| Phone unreviewed / uncertain events | 11 | 12 | +1, inside the C-block repetition the reviewer left unlabelled |
| 022535Z (never used for derivation) expected words | 4 | 5 | C-block HELLO split from LUNCH1 |

**Reading:**
- **Gains.** The rule recovers consecutive words wherever S2 produces a second ACTIVE run:
  - +27 on held-out signers (independent);
  - +1 in 022535Z (independent);
  - +1 in SEP27 and +3 in 023004Z (the session the rule was derived from).
- **Duplicates.** It splits genuine signs rarely: 7 of about 1,500 on CAL, 1 of about 900 on DEV,
  0 on the phone.
- **Cost.** Its cost is not new false activations. It cuts false activity that S2 already
  produces into more pieces: only 1 of the 11 extra CAL non-sign events is a new activation.
  The pre-registered +10% CAL event limit counted those fragments, which is why the rule failed it.
- **Verdict.** Keep the rule as the event-separation candidate, paired with an S2 that
  discriminates better. With S2-R2 it changes nothing (S2-R2 never re-enters ACTIVE), so it
  cannot help alone.

## 5. Whole-event admission: not supported by the evidence

Source: `admission.py` → `admission_ARM_A_E10__CONFIRM_BOUNDARY_70{,__p1}.json`; `phone_loso_gate.py`.

**Separability.** AUC of genuine/correct events against non-sign events:

| Evidence at commit time | CAL P1 (hands-visible, reviewed) | Held-out validation signers* | Phone (19 vs 10) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| WORD top-1 probability | 0.65 | 0.64 | 0.91 |
| WORD v2 top-1 probability | 0.73 | 0.67 | 0.88 |
| Accepted-hand fraction | 0.68 | 0.46 | 0.47 |
| S2 peak P(ACTIVE) | 0.88 | 0.62 | 0.57 |
| Wrist travel / handshape change | 0.78 / 0.48 | 0.59 / 0.60 | 0.51 / 0.43 |
| Frozen and v2 agree | 0.75 | 0.62 | 0.60 |

\* The held-out validation non-sign events are mostly sign fragments (§2), so this column
measures fragment rejection, not ordinary movement.

**Pre-registered rule** (keep ≥ 95% of calibration genuine and correct-word events, then remove
the most non-sign):

| Rule | Selected threshold | Held-out correct kept | Phone correct kept | Phone confirmed non-sign kept |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| WORD confidence | 0.0 (none possible) | 100% | 19/19 | 10/10 |
| Hand-evidence floor | 0.55 | 95% | 18/19 | 10/10 |
| Logistic, all evidence (CAL P1 calibration) | 0.16 | 97% | 15/19 | 8/10 |

**Phone-domain check** (confidence threshold chosen on the other sessions; development-exposed):
- correct words 19 → 14;
- confirmed non-sign 10 → 5;
- the threshold swings from 0.50 to 0.95 between folds.

**Conclusion:**
- A completed event does not carry enough reliable evidence to separate ordinary hands-visible
  movement from signing.
  - Recognizer confidence works only as a precision/recall dial: it removes roughly one false
    event per correct word lost.
  - The other evidence does not transfer from calibration data to the phone.
  - A learned classifier on 10 phone negatives is not credible.
- The confidence gate stays a product policy option (calibrated in the previous milestone), not
  a reliability fix. Its system-level effect is in §7.
- The smallest evidence that could change this verdict: at least 30 reviewed hands-visible
  non-sign events from at least 3 people who are not in any training set.

## 6. S2-R3: does more hands-visible training evidence fix discrimination?

**Experiment ("Arm P").** The Arm A recipe, seed and selection rule were kept unchanged. Added
to FIT: the reviewed phone sessions (PRE on confirmed non-sign, ACTIVE on reviewed genuine signs,
IGNORE elsewhere; 9–11 chunks per fold).
- Five leave-one-session-out models were trained, plus one model on all sessions.
- Each session is scored only by the model that did not train on it.
- 022535Z (protocol labels) is scored by the all-sessions model, which never saw it.
- Sources: `model/config/recognition_upgrade/s2_r3_train_03.json`, `s2_r3_train.py --holdout`,
  `evaluate_arm_p.py` → `arm_p_loso.json`. Each training run takes about 12 minutes.

| Held-out session | Arm A e10 + CB: correct / non-sign | Arm P + CB (selected epoch) | Arm P + CB (epoch 10) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SEP27 (deliberate non-sign protocol) | 4/7 / 7 | 4/7 / **2** | 4/7 / 3 |
| OCT15 | 4/4 / 2 | **0/4** / 1 | 2/4 / 0 |
| SEP23 independent | 3/6 / 0 | 2/6 / 1 | 1/6 / 0 |
| SEP23 representative | 3/6 / 0 | 3/6 / 0 | 2/6 / 0 |
| Shadow 023004Z | 5/6 / 1 | **2/6** / 0 | 2/6 / 0 |
| **Total** | **19/29 / 10** | 11/29 / 4 | 11/29 / 3 |
| 022535Z (all-sessions model): expected / ordinary-movement words | 5 / 2 | 2 / 0 | — |

**Reading:**
- **It suppresses movement, but also signing.** Arm P removes ordinary-movement events where
  they occur (SEP27: 7 → 2). It also silences genuine HELLO/HOME from the same people (OCT15
  4 → 0; 023004Z 5 → 2). Missed signs rise from 7 to 13.
- **It is a more conservative S2, not a more discriminating one.** At 9 Tier-2 words it has
  4 non-sign events, against 6 for Arm A e21 @ 0.8. That is a slightly better curve, but
  nowhere near keeping the consecutive-sign gains.
- **The hypothesis needs refining.** "Missing hands-visible negatives" is necessary but not
  sufficient. With a handful of phone sessions from essentially one or two people, the 378 S2
  inputs do not separate this signer's signing from this signer's movement. The model learns
  "this phone domain → quiet", the same overlap the phone AUC of 0.77–0.83 showed.
- **What would plausibly work:** person-diverse negatives AND positives in the same capture
  conditions, so the only stable cue left is the signing itself. A larger sweep of weights or
  epochs on these five development-exposed sessions would be fitting the gate, so it was stopped.
- **Arm P is not adopted.** Its models stay in the evidence root as research evidence.

## 8. Answers

1. **Approach.** One event-level ledger shared by every question, labelled only from review.
   Then three experiments: event-level attribution of the confirm-boundary rule, whole-event
   admission under a pre-registered calibration rule, and a leave-one-session-out retrain of
   S2-R3. Each was the smallest test that could change a decision.
2. **Implemented and tested.**
   - `event_reliability.py` (ledger, features, categories) with 4 unit tests.
   - CAL streams now keep raw and negative family (RNG untouched).
   - `s2_r3_train.py --holdout` with the reviewed-phone FIT option and config `s2_r3_train_03.json`.
   - Probes under `docs/audit/probes/event_reliability/`.
   - Six Arm P trainings.
   - The three orphan outputs now have a script.
3. **Confirm-boundary contribution.**
   - Held-out continuous: +27 correct words (125 → 152), merged events 100 → 79, 1 duplicate.
   - Phone: +4 words, 0 added non-sign events.
   - Its CAL cost is fragmentation of existing false activity (1 new activation in 11).
   - Keep it as the event-separation candidate. It needs an S2 that re-enters ACTIVE, so it does
     nothing with S2-R2.
4. **Whole-event admission.** It does not reject ordinary movement without losing words: no
   evidence separates them on held-out data (§5). The held-out-calibrated confidence gate is a
   usable precision setting. On top of Arm A + CB it keeps 16 of 19 reviewed phone words and
   cuts non-sign events 10 → 3 and unreviewed events 12 → 5. It costs about a third of the
   correct words on unseen signers (265 → 173), which is still above production (135).
5. **S2-R3.** It needs more training evidence, and of a specific kind: person-diverse
   hands-visible non-sign AND continuous signing captured together. Adding the existing
   sessions' negatives made S2 quieter overall, not more discriminating (§6).
6. **Against the frozen baseline** (same recordings):
   - Reviewed phone correct words: 12 → 19 ungated (16 gated).
   - Confirmed non-sign events: 6 → 10 ungated (3 gated).
   - Continuous distinct signs: 2 → 9 of 11.
   - Held-out continuous correct words: 78 → 152.
   - 022535Z: 2 → 5 expected words.
7. **Regressions and limitations:**
   - Ungated Arm A + CB adds confirmed non-sign events (6 → 10) and fires about 10× more on CAL
     hands-visible P1 (7 → 80).
   - The gate costs about 35% of the held-out correct words.
   - TABLE stays missed: 6 of the 7 misses have no accepted hands.
   - All phone evidence is development-exposed, from one phone and few people; 023004Z labels
     are unblinded.
   - Arm A has no on-device parity run yet.

**Next most valuable action.** One blinded capture session with 3 or more people on the phone,
recorded with the existing shadow build:
- isolated and hands-up continuous protocol signs;
- about 3 minutes each of the five non-sign behaviours;
- review by someone who has not seen model output.

This single session supplies all three missing pieces:
- the first independent phone test of Arm A + CB, with and without the gate (go/no-go for an
  Android shadow);
- person-split material for an S2-R3 retrain that can learn signing rather than session;
- at least 30 non-sign events, which is enough to reopen whole-event admission honestly.

In parallel, and cheap: export Arm A e10 to TFLite and run the existing S2 parity test on the
device, so the shadow build is ready for that session.
